About

BelloSounds Records is an independent record label and creative space for deep house, soulful sounds and everything that feels at home somewhere in between.

It started with a simple feeling: there should still be room for music made with care, without having to chase every new trend. So we decided to make some room of our own.

Vinyl, late-night radio and club music have been part of our world since the ’90s. They taught us to listen closely, to enjoy the space between sounds, and to appreciate a record that stays with you long after it ends. But we're not trying to bring those years back. We're interested in what we can make now.

That curiosity takes different forms. Neel Miles and Nick Evan follow their own paths, with different moods and stories. They don't need to sound alike to belong here.

We're a small, independent project, still finding new connections through records, mixes and the people who take the time to listen. There's no grand plan to explain every step. Just music we care about, and a place to share it.

No rush. Just resonance.
